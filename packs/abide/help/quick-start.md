# Quick start

Fifteen minutes. By the end you will know what this holds and what to do
tomorrow.

## Sign in

Your administrator gives you a username, a password and the address. The first
person ever to open a new install creates the administrator account.

## Look at Get ready

Six stages, 29 steps, from setting the product up to keeping the programme true
between reviews. Each step says what to do, why it matters, which part of the
Rule it satisfies, and has a button to the screen where you do it.

Two kinds of step:

- **Checked for you.** The product looks at its own data and turns them green.
- **Ticked by you.** Things no software can see, such as whether a policy was
  approved.

Do them in order. The order is not arbitrary: the risk analysis comes before
everything because it decides what the rest of the work should be.

## Open a requirement

**Requirements** holds all 72 items of the Security Rule and the Breach
Notification Rule.

Open any one and the top of the screen tells you, in plain words:

- what it is
- what to do about it
- what an investigator will look for
- which records prove it

Above that you will see **Required** or **Addressable**. Required means do it.
Addressable means do it, or do something equivalent, or write down why neither
is reasonable for you — and the written reason is the part that counts.

## Set an owner on a few

Pick five requirements and give each one an owner and a status. That is the
whole working pattern of this product: a person, a state, and the evidence
attached.

## Load the examples

**Settings** → example data fills **Assets** and **Risks** with what most
healthcare organisations have: the EHR, the billing company, the laptops, the
fax-to-email route, ransomware, phishing, the curious member of staff.

They are there to argue with. Delete what is not yours.

## Two things worth knowing today

**Nothing here leaves your server.** No cloud, no telemetry, no licence check.

**There is no HIPAA certificate.** Nobody can certify you. What you are
building is a file that answers questions when they are asked.

## Then

Read **Getting ready, in order** for the programme, and
**The first day of a suspected breach** before you need it.
